## Research claim ceiling

Historical Eye Detected metrics are preserved in
[`history/EYE_DETECTED_POC_V2_3_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE.md`](history/EYE_DETECTED_POC_V2_3_HISTORICAL_EVIDENCE.md).
They are not current R1 evidence. Synthetic smoke is engineering evidence. No source supports diagnosis, clinical
utility, OCT-confirmed DME from fundus/UWF, or lesion localization from MIL attention.

R0 must record foundation-model pretraining overlap for C1 and C2. Until that is resolved, an external dataset cannot
be described as a clean generalization test.
